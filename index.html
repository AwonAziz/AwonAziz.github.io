About Me

I'm Awon Aziz, a Machine Learning & AIOps Engineer based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan. I design and deploy AI-driven solutions across multi-cloud environments — building systems that don't just run, but adapt, monitor themselves, and recover automatically.

My work spans the full MLOps lifecycle: from data pipelines and model training through deployment, real-time monitoring, and continuous optimisation. I'm especially interested in the intersection of AI and infrastructure — where machine intelligence makes systems more resilient.

Outside of production systems, I build things that interest me: music visualisers, 3D browser games, and collaborative ML experiments. Active learner of emerging cloud and AI technologies, and looking to contribute to open-source.
